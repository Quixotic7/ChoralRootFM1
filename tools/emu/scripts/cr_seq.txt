# A. The cycle: the view, SEQ: the editor (the PLAY menu, layer 8, under it); SEQ: the PLAY menu; SEQ: the view. SEQ's
#    LED: lit in the PLAY menu, dim on the view. The click never toggles.

# E. GLO held + SEQ: the click on / off, no Options, no editor.
# F. The FX layer, the Options: SEQ changes nothing.
# G. A take armed: SEQ is refused ("end the take first"), the view stays.
# H. SEQ held on the view: the SEQ layer, as before. In the editor SEQ held opens the SEQ layer too (the owner, after the
#    first drums build: "when you hold seq from the sequencer it takes you to the drums"), the editor closed, and
#    PRESETS turned with SEQ still down is that layer's (the beat on), never the slot or the sound; turned inside the
#    hold time it is the editor's step cursor and the editor stays.
# I. In the editor LOOP held: the PLAY menu (the editor closed, the menu open), PRESETS with LOOP still down: the
#    menu's slot. A LOOP tap in the editor: play / stop, the editor stays. LOOP held with no knob: the PLAY menu.
