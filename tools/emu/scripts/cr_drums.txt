# ChoralRoot: the drums (the DRUMS plan; docs/MANUAL.md "The drums"). The SEQ layer (SEQ held) opens on its preset page:
# Metronome, one page. ALGORITHM +1: Drums (the pages Beat Kick Snare Hats Mix after it); KNOB 3 +1: kit X Deep, -1: X
# Club. SELECT: Beat (the map, the lanes); KNOB 1 / 2: Map X / Y, KNOB 3 Density; SELECT: Kick, KNOB 3 its Character;
# SELECT to the end (Mix), KNOB 1: the kick's FX send; back to the preset page. A root (D4 with A#3: D major) plays in
# the layer. PRESETS +1: the beat armed ("drums armed"): no sound with the transport stopped. OCT- closes the layer.
# LOOP with no loop: the drums alone ("drums play"); LOOP: they stop ("drums stop"); LOOP again: they run. The PLAY
# menu: Length 1 bar. REC with the drums running mid-bar: the count-in is the rest of their bar (their step goes on),
# a chord, the take starts on their bar 1 and commits after a bar: the loop plays, the drums with it; LOOP: both stop.
# GLO + SEQ: off ("drums off"). The WAV: silence while only armed, the drums alone after LOOP.
