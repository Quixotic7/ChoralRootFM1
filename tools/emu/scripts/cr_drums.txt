# ChoralRoot: the drums (the DRUMS plan; docs/MANUAL.md "The beat"). The SEQ layer (SEQ held) opens on its preset page:
# Metronome, one page. KNOB 1 +1: Drums (the pages Beat Kick Snare Hats after it); KNOB 4 +1: kit X Deep, -1: X Club.
# SELECT: Beat (the map, the lanes); KNOB 1 / 2: Map X / Y, KNOB 3 Density; SELECT: Kick, KNOB 3 its Character;
# SELECT to the end (Hats) and back to the preset page. SEQ tapped in the layer: the beat on ("drums on"), OCT-
# closes it. With no loop the drums wait (no step, no hit). REC + a chord + REC: a 1-bar loop; the drums play on its
# grid from bar 1 (hits, a step), the home page's chip; LOOP: the loop stops and the drums with it. GLO + SEQ: off
# ("drums off"). The WAV: the take's chord and the drums under the loop.
