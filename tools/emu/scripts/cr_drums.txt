# ChoralRoot: the drums (the DRUMS plan; docs/MANUAL.md "The drums"). The SEQ layer (SEQ held) opens on its preset page:
# Metronome, one page. ALGORITHM +1: Drums (the pages Beat Kick Snare Hats Mix after it); KNOB 3 +1: kit Deep, -1: X
# Club. SELECT: Beat (the map, the lanes); KNOB 1 / 2: Map X / Y, KNOB 3 Density; SELECT: Kick, KNOB 3 its Character;
# SELECT to the end (Mix), KNOB 1: the kick's FX send; back to the preset page. A root (D4 with A#3: D major) plays in
# the layer. PRESETS +1: the beat on ("drums on"): with no loop they start at once, free on the tempo. OCT- closes
# the layer. REC + a chord + REC: a 1-bar loop; the drums go on, on its grid from its bar 1; LOOP: the loop stops and
# the drums run on (they were on before it). GLO + SEQ: off ("drums off"). The WAV: the drums alone before the take.
