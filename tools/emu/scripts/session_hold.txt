# test_persist.sh, saving and sound (0.18). MAJ + D4 held; KNOB 1 +2 (the voicing) while it sounds; 3 s later the
# chord still sounds: nothing written (dump 1). SAVE held under the chord: the global save writes at once, it is the
# player's own press (dump 2: session saves 1; the erase's 45 ms of silence is in the dump's "audio stalled" line).
# An Option changed under the same chord (Options, KNOB 1: Play Style): the automatic part waits, as ever: 3 s later
# nothing written (dump 3); the chord let go: written once the tail is over (dump 4).
wait 300
key F3 down
wait 40
btn D4 down
wait 300
knob KNOB1 +2
wait 3000
expect sound
dump                          # 1: held: nothing written
btn SAVE 900                  # the global save, into the sounding chord
wait 300
expect sound
dump                          # 2: written: session saves 1
btn GLO
wait 200
btn OCT+ 30
knob KNOB1 +1                 # Play Style: Advanced (an Option: automatic)
wait 170
btn GLO
wait 3000
expect sound
dump                          # 3: the chord still sounds: the Option not written yet
btn D4 up
key F3 up
wait 7000
dump                          # 4: quiet: written by itself
quit
