# FM TONE's RATIO B heard (docs/EDITOR.md §6, the Digitone's hands: a detent steps B2, the fast hand on top of the
# fraction; OPT / Shift held steps B1, the bottom): 02 BELL, EDIT: SYN 1 Algo to 2 (the manual's: A > C on X,
# B2 > B1 on Y, B1 a direct carrier); SYN 1+ MIX +63 (Y only); ENV A/B row B: B LEV 0, then up to a small index
# (B1's phase runs forward: tools/emu/wavpitch.py counts its pitch, B2 colours it); back on SYN 1: RATIO B to a known
# pair: GLO held + KNOB 4 -30 (B1 0.25), KNOB 4 -30 (0.25/0.25), GLO held + KNOB 4 +3 (B1 1.00: 0.25/1.00), KNOB 4 +4
# (B2 2.00: "2.00/1.00"); a C4 (MIDI, part 0); KNOB 4 +1: B2 3.00 (the TOP number: "3.00/1.00", shot), C4 again (the
# same pitch, another timbre); GLO held + KNOB 4 +1: B1 2.00 ("3.00/2.00", "fine . B1" in the title line: shot), C4
# again (an octave up); GLO held + KNOB 4 +30: B1 stops at 16.00 (3.00/16.00, the longest denominator: shot), -30: at
# 0.25. tools/emu/test_cr.sh reads the "deep:" lines, the shots and the WAV's notes (wavpitch.py: peak and centroid)
wait 300
btn EDIT down
wait 400
btn E4
wait 300
btn EDIT up
btn OCT+                      # FM TONE (01 EP)
wait 300
knob PRESETS +1               # 02 BELL
wait 600
btn EDIT                      # OSC: SYN 1
wait 400
knob KNOB1 -8                 # Algo 1
wait 100
knob KNOB1 +1                 # Algo 2: B2 > B1 on Y
wait 100
knob SELECT +1                # row B: SYN 1+
wait 100
knob KNOB4 +127               # MIX +63
wait 100
knob SELECT -1                # row A: SYN 1
wait 100
btn ENV                       # ENV A/B
wait 300
knob SELECT +1                # row B
wait 100
knob KNOB4 -40                # B LEV 0
wait 100
knob KNOB4 +10                # .. B LEV 60, a moderate index (B2 colours B1, B1's pitch still the peak)
wait 100
knob SELECT -1
wait 100
btn FX                        # OSC: back on SYN 1, row A
wait 600
btn GLO down
wait 50
knob KNOB4 -30                # SHIFT: B1 to 0.25
wait 50
btn GLO up
wait 50
knob KNOB4 -30                # B2 to 0.25: 0.25/0.25
wait 50
btn GLO down
wait 50
knob KNOB4 +3                 # SHIFT: B1 1.00: 0.25/1.00
wait 50
btn GLO up
wait 50
knob KNOB4 +4                 # B2 2.00: 2.00/1.00
wait 200
shot build/emu/test/cr_quad_ratiob_b0.ppm
wait 1000
midi 90 3C 64                 # note 1: C4 at 2.00/1.00
wait 700
midi 80 3C 00
wait 1500
knob KNOB4 +1                 # B2 3.00: 3.00/1.00 (the top number)
wait 200
shot build/emu/test/cr_quad_ratiob_b1.ppm
wait 1000
midi 90 3C 64                 # note 2: the same pitch, B2 another timbre
wait 700
midi 80 3C 00
wait 1500
btn GLO down
wait 50
knob KNOB4 +1                 # SHIFT: B1 1.00 -> 2.00, B2 kept
wait 200
shot build/emu/test/cr_quad_ratiob_b2.ppm
btn GLO up
wait 1000
midi 90 3C 64                 # note 3: C4 an octave up
wait 700
midi 80 3C 00
wait 1500
btn GLO down
wait 50
knob KNOB4 +30                # B1 up to its end: 16.00
wait 200
shot build/emu/test/cr_quad_ratiob_b16.ppm
knob KNOB4 -30                # .. down to its end: 0.25
wait 50
btn GLO up
wait 3000                     # (BELL's release: every voice ends)
dump
quit
