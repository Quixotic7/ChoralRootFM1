# ChoralRoot: the screen when idle (docs/INTEGRATION.md section 5). A chord, then no input: the view, the logo after
# 3 min, the screen off after 10 min (DISPOFF + the backlight off: the shot black, no LCD write for a further minute).
# A key wakes it and plays (the view back, the chord sounding); 10 min more: off again, a knob turn wakes it and
# changes the value (KNOB 1: voicing 2). A loop playing does not keep it on: off 10 min later, the loop still playing.
