# ChoralRoot 0.20: Options > 6th with 7th (docs/CHORD-NAMES.md "The 6th above a seventh"): In place, the default (the
# Orchid's: the 6th 9 semitones above the root, under the seventh), or Octave up (a chord with the 6 AND a seventh
# plays its 6th 21 semitones above the root: a 13th). The entry after Secret Chords (3).
# Chord keys: F3 = 6, G3 = 7 (m7), A3 = M7, A#3 = MAJ, C4 = 9; root A4 (69). Read in the dump's "cr: voices" line (the
# engine's name and notes), the "key leds" line and the "sixth:" line. tools/emu/test_cr.sh reads the dumps by number:
#   1 In place: A + 7 + 6 = 69 73 76 78 79          2 the entry, In place     3 KNOB 1 +1: Octave up
#   4 the chord of 1 was held through the change: its notes as they were (a sounding chord keeps its notes)
#   5 released: silent (its note-offs were its own)
#   6 Octave up: A + 7 + 6 = 69 73 76 79 90         7 A + M7 + 6 = 69 73 76 80 90
#   8 A + 6 (no seventh): 69 73 76 78, as ever       9 A + 7 + 6 + 9 = 69 73 76 79 83 90 (the 13th above the 9th)
#   10 A + 7 + 6 held, KNOB 1 -1: the voicing takes the top note, the 13th, down an octave: 69 73 76 78 79
#   11 KNOB 1 +1: the voicing back: 69 73 76 79 90
#   12 KNOB 1 -1 in the Options: In place again     13 A + 7 + 6 = 69 73 76 78 79 again     14 silent
keys classic                  # (0.25.1: FM-1 is the default; this script was written for the Classic chord keys)
wait 500
btn A#3 down                  # MAJ + 7 + 6, A4
btn G3 down
btn F3 down
btn A4 down
wait 400
dump                          # 1
shot build/emu/test/cr_sixth_off.ppm
btn GLO 80                    # Options > 6th with 7th, the chord still held
wait 300
knob SELECT +1
btn OCT+ 30
knob SELECT +2
wait 270
dump                          # 2
shot build/emu/test/cr_sixth_opt_off.ppm
knob KNOB1 +1
wait 300
dump                          # 3
shot build/emu/test/cr_sixth_opt_on.ppm
knob KNOB1 +3                 # (past the end: stays Octave up)
wait 200
btn GLO 80
wait 400
dump                          # 4
btn A4 up
wait 2500
dump                          # 5
btn A4 down                   # the same keys, Octave up
wait 400
dump                          # 6
shot build/emu/test/cr_sixth_on.ppm
btn A4 up
btn G3 up
wait 300
btn A3 down                   # MAJ + M7 + 6
btn A4 down
wait 400
dump                          # 7
btn A4 up
btn A3 up
wait 300
btn A4 down                   # MAJ + 6: no seventh
wait 400
dump                          # 8
btn A4 up
wait 300
btn G3 down                   # MAJ + 7 + 6 + 9
btn C4 down
btn A4 down
wait 400
dump                          # 9
btn A4 up
btn C4 up
wait 300
btn A4 down                   # MAJ + 7 + 6, then the voicing one step down
wait 300
knob KNOB1 -1
wait 400
dump                          # 10
knob KNOB1 +1
wait 300
dump                          # 11
btn A4 up
btn G3 up
btn F3 up
btn A#3 up
wait 600
btn GLO 80                    # In place again (the menu opens on the entry it was left on)
wait 300
btn OCT+ 30
knob SELECT +2
knob KNOB1 -1
wait 270
dump                          # 12
btn GLO 80
wait 500
btn A#3 down
btn G3 down
btn F3 down
btn A4 down
wait 400
dump                          # 13
btn A4 up
btn F3 up
btn G3 up
btn A#3 up
wait 2500
dump                          # 14
quit
