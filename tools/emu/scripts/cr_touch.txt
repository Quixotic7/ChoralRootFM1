# ChoralRoot 0.22: the keys' velocity and aftertouch in the emulator (docs/VELOCITY.md; tools/emu/emu_fw.c
# emu_touch_feed: a key the script holds "down VEL" lands at the driven reading the map gives that velocity, a "lean"
# presses it harder). Key Touch Fixed (a new unit): D4 at 30 plays at Options > Velocity, 100, at once. Options >
# Key Touch (entry 5) to Velocity + Aftertouch: D4 at 30 plays at 30 (the trace "touch: key 9 vel 30 sensed"),
# about 9 ms after the key went down; at 120, 120; G5 (a top key, no converter) through the PA10 probe's model at 64
# ("touch: key 26 vel 67 sensed": the probe's curve, quantised); a lean of 60 % on a held D4 after it has settled: aftertouch up to ~73
# ("touch: at N"; 60 % of the span, smoothed), eased to 0: back to 0. Velocity Curve +3 (entry 7): the same press
# (30 on Velo's scale) reads 15.
keys classic                  # (0.25.1: FM-1 is the default; this script was written for the Classic chord keys)
wait 600
btn D4 down 30
wait 300
btn D4 up
wait 300
btn GLO 80                    # Options
wait 300
knob SELECT +2
btn OCT+ 30
knob SELECT +1  # Key Touch
wait 170
knob KNOB1 +2                 # Velocity + Aftertouch
wait 200
btn GLO 80
wait 500
btn D4 down 30
wait 300
btn D4 up
wait 300
btn D4 down 120
wait 300
btn D4 up
wait 300
btn G5 down 64
wait 300
btn G5 up
wait 300
btn D4 down 80
wait 500                      # the rest settles (250 ms), then the lean
lean D4 60
wait 400
dump                          # 1: pressure on
lean D4 0
wait 400
dump                          # 2: pressure off
btn D4 up
wait 300
btn GLO 80
wait 300
knob SELECT +2  # Velocity Curve (entry 7)
wait 200
knob KNOB1 +3                 # +3
wait 200
btn GLO 80
wait 500
btn D4 down 30
wait 300
btn D4 up
wait 500
dump                          # 3
quit
