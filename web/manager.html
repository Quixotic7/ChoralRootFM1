    Your FM-1

    Factory presets:

      Click selects; shift-click a range, cmd / ctrl-click adds one. Drag rows within a pool to reorder (onto another sound: they swap places; onto a factory preset: it takes that place), from the library onto the FM-1 to Place (several onto a factory preset: they replace it and the factory presets after it, as Replace factory does), from the FM-1 onto the library to Keep. ★ marks a favourite as the FM-1 does (C / B: typed Chord or Bass; marks are set on the FM-1, SAVE > Favorite): a factory preset's mark stays with its place, so a sound placed over it shows it.
      Mouse, or the computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P; Z / X octave. Plain notes, velocity 100, on channel 1 (the chord part): the FM-1 plays them as a MIDI instrument, so its own chord-quality keys are not part of what you hear.
      Device not found?

      Check the USB cable (a data cable, directly on the computer, not through a hub), close other apps that use MIDI (the editor, a DAW holding the port), switch the FM-1 on: the page connects by itself when the port "ChoralRoot FM-1" appears (a Mac may keep calling it "FM-1": that works too). The FM-1 must run ChoralRoot (the installer puts it on).

      Auditioning: selecting a row loads it on the unit (SysEx SELECT: the engine and the position); after a Place, Rename, Swap or Delete the row at that place is loaded again, so a change is heard at once. A firmware without SELECT gets a program change instead, which plays the row only when the unit is already on its engine. Writes stop the sound for about 45 ms per object; a write while a loop plays is refused: stop the loop on the FM-1 first.

      Try the page without a device: ?sim=1 (a simulated FM-1; the keyboard shows what it would send).
