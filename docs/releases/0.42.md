ChoralRoot 0.42 brings **progressions** (GitHub issue 64), the way the Ripchord plug-in does them: a key plays a whole
chord stored for it. Sixteen progressions live on the FM-1, each up to 48 keys of chords of up to 8 notes; six of
ChoralRoot's own come with it, and the device manager imports Ripchord presets and lets you write your own. Your
settings, sounds, loops and favorites come through the update.

## Keyboard: Prog

Hold KEY, SELECT to page 2 and turn KNOB 2 past Full to **Prog**. Prog lies over the Chords keyboard:

- The **root keys** from C4 up play the progression: C4 its first chord, C#4 its second, up to G5, the 20th. A root
  key with no chord is silent.
- The **chord keys** F3 to B3 keep their jobs: a root pressed while a chord key is held, or latched by LOCK, plays your
  own chord as ever; a root pressed alone plays the progression's. So one hand plays the progression and drops into
  chords of its own.
- **OCT- and OCT+** slide the root keys 12 chords along a longer progression (keys 13 to 32, 25 to 44, 37 to 48);
  they do not change the octave there. The Chord view's top right shows where you are (`keys 13-32`).
- **Tap KEY** to put the progression aside (the plain Chords keyboard) and tap again to bring it back. With Keyboard
  on Chords or Full, the tap is Key Mode's on / off as before.
- The Chord view names each progression chord from its notes (`Em9`, `C7/E`, `Gdim7/B`), lists all of them with the
  bass in orange, and its top line says which progression and key, in purple (`Neo Soul · 5`). The Keyboard view is
  unchanged.
- The bass part, the performance modes (Strum, Arp, Pattern ..), glide and MIDI out play the progression's chords as
  any chord; the voicing (KNOB 1) turns them and Transpose moves them.

## KEY layer page 3

A third page (SELECT): **KNOB 1** picks the progression (its name big, the ones before and after above and below),
**KNOB 2** Velocity (50 to 150 % of your touch), **KNOB 3** Humanize (a random spread on each note's velocity, up to
40 either side at 100 %), **KNOB 4** Transpose (-24 to +24 semitones, the progression alone, on top of page 1's). The
KEY layer now opens on the page you left it on.

## The progressions on the unit

- Slots 1 to 6: **Pop Four**, **Jazz ii-V-I**, **Neo Soul**, **Gospel Walk**, **Minor Epic**, **Dorian Days**, eight
  chords each. Slots 7 to 16 are empty.
- Prog, the progression, Velocity, Humanize and Transpose are kept as the unit is left (no SAVE needed).
- **Loops** keep which progression and key a chord came from: edit the progression and the loop plays the new chord.
  A loop keeps a Transpose of -7 to +7 as it is; beyond, the notes' names and not the octave.
- **MIDI Keys** (Options): its notes play the keys they press with the same rule (note 60 is the first chord), and
  notes 80 to 107 reach chords 21 to 48 directly.

## In the device manager

The **Progressions** view (beside Sounds & Loops) reads the unit's sixteen, shows how full they are (3.7 KB in all),
and sends them back with undo:

- **Import Ripchord presets**: drop `.rpc` files (many at once). Gaps stay silent keys, keys past 48 are left out, and
  a chord of more than 8 notes loses its doubled notes first, then middle ones; each preset's notes say what changed.
- **Edit and create**: the name, the chord on the top keyboard (click notes, at most 8), the keybed below (the FM-1's
  or the SMK-37's) to pick a key, Listen, Copy / Paste / Clear, and Export as `.rpc`.
- **Factory set** puts the six back. A backup holds the progressions; Options > Flash Data erases them.

## Also

- In the KEY layer with its keys on pick, **C4 and C#4** set the tonic (C and C#) as the other roots do; on the FM-1
  layout they did nothing there.

## Installing

- The web installer's default is 0.42: press Install. Options > System > Version reads `ChoralRoot 0.42`.
- **From 0.27 to 0.41**, nothing needs doing: your sounds, loops, favorites and settings are all there, and the six
  progressions are waiting.
- **From 0.21 or older**: install 0.29.2 first and switch the FM-1 on once to keep 0.21's sounds (the installer asks;
  [0.30.md](0.30.md)).
- **A Single core package**: the web installer's **Advanced > Hardware fallback**. Options > System > Version then
  reads `ChoralRoot 0.42 1-core`.
- **From another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader and
  `choralroot-0.42.fwsc`. M-Flasher writes the firmware alone: the unit starts with PRESETS MISSING until ChoralRoot's
  own installer has run once.
- **On the SMK-37** (0.42-smk37): everything above on its 37 keys from C3, its pads as the chord keys; MIDI Keys is
  not on the SMK-37. SMK37.md "What to test".
- **Signed builds**: GitHub built these packages from the tag's source and signed them; [0.26.1.md](0.26.1.md)
  "Checking a package" says how to check one (`choralroot-0.42.fwsc`).

## Going back

- **To 0.41**: the installer offers it under Other versions. Your settings, sounds, loops and favorites stay; 0.41
  has no progressions (it leaves them stored for 0.42), and **a loop slot holding a progression chord reads as empty
  there** (it is still there for 0.42, unless 0.41 records into that slot).
- **To another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader (MANUAL.md
  "Switching firmwares"); back up ChoralRoot's data in the installer first.
- **To the stock firmware completely**: M-VAVE's own updater, M-Updater.
