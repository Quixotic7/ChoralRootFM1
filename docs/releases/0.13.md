**The brick fix** (Felucca 1.0.3.1's #61): a 0.9-era DIGITAL preset with INDEX 0 made the boot-time DIGITAL → FM6 conversion divide by zero, which the JieLi compiler hoists ahead of its guard; the trap fired before the screen came up, and after three such boots the FM-1 fell into ROM boot ("WL82 UBOOT1.00"). The divides are now computed with non-zero divisors and the chip's divide trap is off, as upstream. This is what bricked a Sloop → ChoralRoot install. Felucca's own regression test for it is in the suite.

**Recovery tooling**: a soft-bricked FM-1 can be restored over plain USB with https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker (Windows or Linux); the installer page's "Device not found?" block explains the symptoms.
