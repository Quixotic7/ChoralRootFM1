The first public beta of ChoralRoot: a Telepathic Orchid-style chord instrument for the M-VAVE FM-1, built on Felucca's engines.

**Playing**
- The Orchid's chord block on the keybed: DIM MIN MAJ SUS on F#3 G#3 A#3 C#4, the extensions 6 m7 M7 9 on F3 G3 A3 C4, B3 = LOCK (latches the chord keys), roots D4–G5 with OCT−/OCT+.
- KNOB 1 is always the chord voicing; Key Mode, three play styles (Simple / Advanced / Free), Extension Addition, secret chords, sticky keys.
- Performance modes Strum, Strum 2, Slop, Arp, Arp 2, Pattern, Harp with their parameters on KNOB 1–4 in the PERF layer; BPM on SELECT.
- Bass (Chords Only / Unison / Single Notes / Solo) on its own part and sound; FX layer with the effect picker.
- The looper: free or 1–16 bar sync with a count-in, quantize, overdub and undo, ten slots in flash, a metronome.
- Layers stay open after a hold; OCT− or HOME closes them.

**Sound**
- Felucca's engines plus ChoralRoot's own four-oscillator VA (basic, morphing and noise oscillator modes, a morphing filter with stereo spread, four envelopes, four LFOs, an eight-slot matrix).
- A bank of chord sounds and basses chosen for chords; 32 user slots with naming.
- The sound editor: section buttons (OSC FILT ENV LFO MOD FX MIX), up to eight parameters a page, wide envelope and filter graphics, oscillator and LFO stacks, the whole mod matrix on one screen, quick modulation mapping (hold ENV or LFO and turn a knob).

**Screens**: the 1960s mod look, one big thing per screen, animated chord names, pickers and stripe meters, the SCOPE and GEEK OUT views, a ChoralRoot splash.

**MIDI**: three streams out (chord / bass / raw) with clock out and start/stop; MIDI in to the parts, clock in, CC and program change.

**Performance pass**: no flash writes while notes sound, a smoothed limiter, faded voice steals, a cheap loop ring.
