ChoralRoot 0.36 gives Key Mode **21 more scales** besides Major and Minor: the modes, the pentatonics, blues, bebop,
whole tone, the diminished scales, some eastern scales and Barry Harris's 6th-diminished. They were asked for in
GitHub issues [#34](https://github.com/Quixotic7/ChoralRootFM1/issues/34) (more scales, "at least all of the modes",
blues and eastern scales) and [#45](https://github.com/Quixotic7/ChoralRootFM1/issues/45) (the 6th-diminished scale).
Thanks to both. Your settings, sounds, loops and favorites are stored as before.

## More scales (new in 0.36)

- **The scales**: Major, Minor, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Locrian, Harmonic Minor, Melodic Minor, Major
  and Minor Pentatonic, Blues, Phrygian Dominant, Double Harmonic, Hirajoshi, Whole Tone, Diminished (whole-half and
  half-whole), Bebop Dominant, Major and Minor, 6-Dim and m6-Dim.
- **Each note of the scale plays the chord built from the scale's own notes**, as in Major and Minor (in C Dorian, F
  plays F and A plays Adim). The 6, m7, M7 and 9 keys add to it as ever, and a chord key held still overrides it.
- **Pentatonics, Blues, Hirajoshi**: the lights, Key plays Notes and Snap use their five or six notes; the chords come
  from the major or minor scale, so the keys between them still play a chord that fits.
- **Whole Tone** plays C+7 chords; the **Diminished** scales play diminished sevenths (and dominant sevenths in
  half-whole); **Bebop**'s passing note plays a diminished seventh.
- **6-Dim and m6-Dim** (Barry Harris): the tonic's 6th chord on C, E, G and A with the key you press in the bass
  (E plays `C6/E`), the diminished seventh on D, F, G# and B, and a key outside the scale its 7b9 chord (C# plays
  `C#7b9`). A loop records these chords as played and plays them back the same way.
- **Picking one**: in the KEY layer (KEY held) KNOB 2 goes through all 23. MIN picks Minor and MAJ Major, as before;
  tapped again, each steps to the next scale of its kind (MIN: Minor, Dorian, Phrygian ...; MAJ: Major, Lydian,
  Mixolydian ...). MIN held with a root still gives a minor key. The top line names the scale (`Key: D dorian`), and
  a long name shows on two lines in the layer's Scale cell.
- **Numerals** count the degrees as Major does for a major scale and as Minor does for a minor one; C6/E reads I.
- **On the SMK-37** (0.36-smk37): the same, with the MIN and MAJ pads.
- The manual's "Scales" table has every scale's chords in C.

## Installing

- The web installer's default is 0.36: press Install. Options > Version reads `ChoralRoot 0.36`.
- **From 0.27 to 0.35**, nothing needs doing: your sounds, loops, favorites and settings are all there, and your key
  stays Major or Minor until you pick another scale.
- **From 0.21 or older**: install 0.29.2 first and switch the FM-1 on once to keep 0.21's sounds (the installer asks;
  [0.30.md](0.30.md)).
- **A Single core package**: the web installer's **Advanced > Hardware fallback**. Options > Version then reads
  `ChoralRoot 0.36 1-core`.
- **From another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader and
  `choralroot-0.36.fwsc`.
- **Signed builds**: GitHub built these packages from the tag's source and signed them; [0.26.1.md](0.26.1.md)
  "Checking a package" says how to check one (`choralroot-0.36.fwsc`).

## Going back

- **To 0.35 or older**: the installer offers it under Other versions. Your settings, sounds,
  loops and favorites stay. **A saved scale it does not know (any but Major and Minor) reads as Major there**, with
  the tonic and Key Mode kept. A global save there stores Major.
- **A loop slot holding a 6-Dim or m6-Dim chord over its 3rd or 5th, or a 7b9 chord, reads as empty on 0.35 and
  older.** It is still stored and plays again on 0.36, unless you record or save into that slot on the older version.
  Loops with the other scales' chords play as recorded.
- **To another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader (MANUAL.md
  "Switching firmwares"); back up ChoralRoot's data in the installer first.
- **To the stock firmware completely**: M-VAVE's own updater, M-Updater.
