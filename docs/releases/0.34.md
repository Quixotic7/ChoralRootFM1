ChoralRoot 0.34 brings **Chord and Bass Perform**: the chords and the bass each have their own performance mode, so
slow sustained chords can sit over an arpeggiated bass, or both can perform at once. The idea is GitHub issue
[#41](https://github.com/Quixotic7/ChoralRootFM1/issues/41), by etyrnal. Your settings, sounds, loops and favorites
are stored as before, and a 0.33 FM-1 plays exactly as it did until you set the bass's mode.

## Chord and Bass Perform (new in 0.34)

- **PERF (ARP) held** is the chord part's PERF layer, as before. **BASS (ENV) held + PERF** is the new **Bass PERF
  layer**: the same modes and knobs in the bass's orange, with `bass perform` at the top. The bass has its own mode
  and its own four parameters for each mode.
- Both pickers now start with **Off**, in front of Strum. A part on Off plays as with the performance off: the chord
  part holds its chord, the bass part its bass note. The chords start on their mode and the bass on Off, which is how
  ChoralRoot played before.
- **A PERF tap is still the one switch**: it turns the performance on and off for both parts.
- The bass performs the chord you play, moved down to where the bass note would be (the BASS layer's Register), on
  the bass sound and the MIDI Bass channel, and it follows the Bass Behaviour (Chords Only, Unison, Single Notes,
  Solo, Split Bass).
- Holding PERF inside the Bass PERF layer switches to the chord's layer.
- **Hold** of either part keeps the chord sounding after you let go; LATCH's gesture turns both off.
- The top right says `Bass Arp` in orange while only the bass performs, and the Chord view's hopping note follows the
  bass's arpeggio then.
- The tempo, LATCH and a MIDI Start are shared: both parts follow them.
- A global save (SAVE held) keeps both parts' modes, Off and parameters.
- **On the SMK-37** (0.34-smk37): CHORD held + ARP opens the Bass PERF layer. The mode pads stay the chord part's.

## The bass on and off at once

- A **BASS (ENV) tap** now turns the bass on and off at once, also under a chord you are holding: its note, or its
  arpeggio, stops, and the next tap brings it back on the same chord. Before, the change waited for the next key.

## The BASS layer's keys play by default

- In the BASS layer (BASS held), the keys now **play**, so you hear the bass while you change it (`bass · keys play`
  at the top). A **GLO** tap in the layer turns them into its shortcuts (`keys pick`, the GLO light on): D4 to G4
  pick the Behaviour. Another tap: they play again. The choice is kept with the settings, as the KEY layer's.

## Installing

- The web installer's default is 0.34: press Install. Options > Version reads `ChoralRoot 0.34`.
- **From 0.27 to 0.33**, nothing needs doing: your sounds, loops, favorites and settings are all there. The bass
  starts on Off in its PERF layer, and the BASS layer's keys play.
- **From 0.21 or older**: install 0.29.2 first and switch the FM-1 on once to keep 0.21's sounds (the installer asks;
  [0.30.md](0.30.md)).
- **A Single core package**: the web installer's **Advanced > Hardware fallback**. Options > Version then reads
  `ChoralRoot 0.34 1-core`.
- **From another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader and
  `choralroot-0.34.fwsc`.
- **Signed builds**: GitHub built these packages from the tag's source and signed them; [0.26.1.md](0.26.1.md)
  "Checking a package" says how to check one (`choralroot-0.34.fwsc`).

## Going back

- **To 0.33**: the installer offers it under Other versions. Your settings, sounds, loops and favorites stay. 0.33
  has no Bass Perform: the bass plays its plain note, and a chord part set to Off performs again with its last mode.
  A global save on 0.33 sets the bass's Perform settings back to their defaults, and in the BASS layer the keys pick
  the Behaviour again.
- **To another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader (MANUAL.md
  "Switching firmwares"); back up ChoralRoot's data in the installer first.
- **To the stock firmware completely**: M-VAVE's own updater, M-Updater.
