ChoralRoot 0.29.2 is **0.29.1 with loops that stay in time with an incoming MIDI clock**. With Options > MIDI Clock
set to In, the loop used to run on the FM-1's own clock at the incoming tempo rounded to a whole BPM, so against a
master at a tempo between two whole BPMs it slowly drifted: about 2 seconds after 10 minutes at 120.4 BPM. It now
keeps time with the clock's pulses themselves. **Nothing else changes**: the sound, the features and the way your
settings, sounds, loops and favorites are stored are 0.29.1's.

## MIDI Clock In (new in 0.29.2)

- **The loop follows the pulses.** Each incoming clock pulse moves the loop on; between pulses the FM-1 fills in, so
  notes still land between them. Measured over 10 minutes at 120.0, 120.4 and 120.7 BPM, the loop stays within 15 ms
  of the master.
- **When the clock stops** (a cable, the master's Stop) the loop plays on at the tempo, and locks again at the next
  pulse. Start and Continue lock it from the pulse that follows them.
- **Not changed**: the arpeggiator and the other tempo-synced parts still follow the incoming tempo as a whole BPM.
- **MIDI Clock Out** was already steady: measured on a unit, its clock and a loop of whole bars stayed together over
  42 bars. A Free loop keeps the length you played, which is rarely a whole number of bars, so it moves against a
  DAW's bars by design: record with Sync set to Bar to stay on the grid.

## Installing

- The web installer's default is 0.29.2: press Install. Options > Version reads `ChoralRoot 0.29.2`.
- **From 0.29.1, 0.29, 0.28 or 0.27** nothing needs doing: your sounds, loops, favorites and settings are all there.
- **An FM-1 with the second flash chip** stuck on 0.27 to 0.29: [0.29.1.md](0.29.1.md) says the way out.
- **A Single core package**: the web installer's **Advanced > Hardware fallback**. Options > Version then reads
  `ChoralRoot 0.29.2 1-core`.
- **Signed builds**: GitHub built these packages from the tag's source and signed them; [0.26.1.md](0.26.1.md)
  "Checking a package" says how to check one (`choralroot-0.29.2.fwsc`).

## Going back to 0.29.1

The installer offers 0.29.1 under Other versions. Nothing changes but MIDI Clock In's timing: 0.29.1 stores
everything the same way, so your settings, sounds, loops and favorites stay, and Options > Version reads
`ChoralRoot 0.29.1` again.
