ChoralRoot 0.43 brings **ChoralRoot on the Ableton Move**: in RemoteFM1 on the Move, ChoralRoot takes the whole Move
(its pads, steps, knobs, wheel, buttons and screen) as its own instrument, the way the FM-1 plays it. On the FM-1 and
the SMK-37 nothing changes: 0.43 plays exactly as 0.42, and your settings, sounds, loops and favorites come through
the update.

## On the Move (RemoteFM1)

Load ChoralRoot in a RemoteFM1 slot, hold its Track button and press the second pad of the top row: the takeover.
Hold the Track button and press the first pad to go back to RemoteFM1's FM-1 layout. Everything below is in the
takeover; the Track buttons and the volume knob stay RemoteFM1's (turning the volume shows its level).

- **The pads.** The two top rows hold the chord keys on the left (DIM MIN MAJ SUS / 6 m7 M7 9) and eight chord
  memories on the right (a key held + a memory pad saves; Delete + a memory pad clears; Menu + a memory pad picks one
  of 8 banks). The two bottom rows play the roots in the layout Menu picks: Keys (a piano octave, the key's tonic
  yellow and its notes lit), Scale, Chromatic, Prog (a progression's chords), Iso (rows a fourth apart) and Iso Key
  (rows a fourth apart, in the key only). Menu + a progression shows its name.
- **The screen** shows the chord big, its notes under it, as the FM-1's Chord view; the loop's step while it plays.
- **The knobs**: Voicing, Bass register, Perf amount, FX send, Key, Scale, Transpose, Tempo. Touch one for its name.
  In a settings page or the sound editor they edit that page; values move faster the faster you turn.
- **The wheel**: the chord sound's preset; click it and it picks the engine; Shift + wheel the bass sound.
- **Shift held**: the pads of the two left columns are the FM-1's buttons, as the screen draws them: KEY, LOCK,
  LATCH, PERF, B.PRF, FX, BASS, METRO. Tap for on / off; hold for that feature's settings (the wheel turns its
  pages). The steps with a lit icon do what the Move's own icon says: 1 the loop slots, 2 Settings, 3 count-in,
  5 tempo, 6 metronome, 7 groove (the loop's swing), 8 the sound editor on the memory pads (EDIT / MEMORY),
  9 key and scale, 10 fixed velocity, 11 Perform, 14 the next empty slot, 15 double the loop, 16 quantize.
- **The sequencer**: the 16 steps are the loop. Tap an empty step: the chord you last played goes in; tap it again:
  gone. Hold a step: it plays (stopped), its root and chord keys light, and the knobs edit its length, chance,
  velocity, ratchet, condition, voicing, transpose and the loop's steps. Copy held + steps copies, Delete held + a step
  cuts, and the next step pastes (as the Move's own sequencer); Delete + Play clears the loop. Loop held: steps 1-10
  pick one of ten loops. Play starts and stops ChoralRoot's loop and RemoteFM1's sequencer together.
- **Back** returns home from any page. **Shift + Delete** is PANIC.
- **Settings** (Shift + step 2): the pads' velocity (Dynamic, Fixed, Soft, Hard; fixed level), the loop (swing,
  quantize, rate, steps, length, count-in, metronome, level, record mode, tempo) and ChoralRoot's play, chord and
  MIDI options. Capture saves them with everything else.

The screen reader speaks all of it.

## Going back

- **To 0.42**: the installer offers it under Other versions. Your settings, sounds, loops and favorites stay; the
  FM-1 plays the same. RemoteFM1's 0.42 ChoralRoot has no takeover.
- **To another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader (MANUAL.md
  "Switching firmwares"); back up ChoralRoot's data in the installer first.
- **To the stock firmware completely**: M-VAVE's own updater, M-Updater.
