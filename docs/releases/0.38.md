ChoralRoot 0.38 makes **Glide** what players asked for: **chords slide into each other**, over **up to 30 seconds**,
and the knob shows the time the slide really takes. It also fixes two notes that could keep sounding after LATCH or
Free. Thanks to jhussung-ux ([#46](https://github.com/Quixotic7/ChoralRootFM1/issues/46)). Your settings, sounds,
loops and favorites are stored as before.

## Glide: chords that slide, up to 30 s (issue 46)

- **Chords glide.** With the voice mode on POLY and Glide above 0, each note of the next chord takes the voice of the
  chord you just let go of that is nearest to it, and slides there from where it is: C E G to D F A slides C to D, E
  to F and G to A; C E G to E G C keeps E and G and slides the C up an octave. The voices go on without a click or a
  gap; their envelopes start again from the level they had. A note added over a chord you hold gets a voice of its
  own, and the held chord does not move. Glide is on the sound editor's MIX group: KNOB 4 in the first lane.
- **Up to 30 s, and the time you see is the time you hear.** Glide now goes from 3 ms to 30 s. Until 0.37 the knob
  read up to 10 s, but no slide took longer than about 1.5 s (RATE far less), and a slow one moved in small steps.
  Slides are smooth now at any speed.
- **Gl. mode** (KNOB 4 in the MIX group's second lane) says what the time means: **RATE** (the default) is the time
  for an octave, so a small step is quicker than a big one; **TIME** is the time for any slide, big or small.
- MONO, LEGATO and UNISON glide as before, for the time shown.
- FM6 keeps its own portamento (the patch's). GRAIN and the sample sounds do not glide chords.
- **Old sounds with Glide above 0 now slide for the time the knob shows, so more slowly than before.** Turn Glide
  down if a sound slides too long. Sounds with Glide at 0 play exactly as before.
- RemoteFM1 and the FM1 plug-in show Glide as a plain number until they are updated for its new display.

## Notes that kept sounding (LATCH and Free)

- **LATCH off with Snap**: a latched chord whose key you had pressed again under Snap (playing another note by then)
  was not let go when LATCH went off, and its sound, with a Bass Perform arp on it, could keep running until you
  pressed PANIC. LATCH off now ends it.
- **Free over the Full keyboard**: with Free sounding the last root again over a note held on the Full keyboard, the
  note could stay on after you let go of its key. The key's release now ends it.

## Also

- **On the SMK-37** (0.38-smk37): all of the above.

## Installing

- The web installer's default is 0.38: press Install. Options > Version reads `ChoralRoot 0.38`.
- **From 0.27 to 0.37**, nothing needs doing: your sounds, loops, favorites and settings are all there.
- **From 0.21 or older**: install 0.29.2 first and switch the FM-1 on once to keep 0.21's sounds (the installer asks;
  [0.30.md](0.30.md)).
- **A Single core package**: the web installer's **Advanced > Hardware fallback**. Options > Version then reads
  `ChoralRoot 0.38 1-core`.
- **From another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader and
  `choralroot-0.38.fwsc`.
- **Signed builds**: GitHub built these packages from the tag's source and signed them; [0.26.1.md](0.26.1.md)
  "Checking a package" says how to check one (`choralroot-0.38.fwsc`).

## Going back

- **To 0.37 or older**: the installer offers it under Other versions. Your settings, sounds, loops and favorites stay.
  Glide reads up to 10 s again but slides for at most about 1.5 s, and POLY chords do not glide.
- **To another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader (MANUAL.md
  "Switching firmwares"); back up ChoralRoot's data in the installer first.
- **To the stock firmware completely**: M-VAVE's own updater, M-Updater.
