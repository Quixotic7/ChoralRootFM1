ChoralRoot 0.27 brings **FX routing**: a Routing page in the FX layer that draws the chorus, the delay, the reverb and
the master filter as boxes on a wire, where you reorder them and choose between **Parallel**, the three send buses
ChoralRoot always had, and **Chain**, one path through the four blocks in the order on the screen, the original
firmware's way. In Parallel the filter can now sit **before** the effects, so a high cut keeps the fizz out of the
reverb and a low cut the bass out of the delay. The web device manager finds an FM-1 that the computer still calls
"FM-1". **Parallel with the filter after the effects, where every FM-1 starts, is 0.26.1's sound bit for bit.** 0.27
replaces 0.26.1 as the web installer's default; updating keeps your settings, sounds, loops and favorites, and going
back to 0.26.1 is possible at any time.

## FX routing (new in 0.27)

Hold FX for the FX layer and turn SELECT to the last page, **Routing**.

- **The picture.** The effects are boxes on a wire from `in` to `out`: Chorus blue, Delay orange, Reverb yellow, the
  master Filter white (hollow while the filter is Off). In Parallel the three effects stand side by side between `in`
  and a sum, the Filter before or after them; in Chain the four are in a row, each feeding the next.
- **Moving a block.** Five black keys are shortcuts on this page: **C#4** Chorus, **D#4** Delay, **F#4** Reverb,
  **G#4** Filter. Hold one and its box lifts above the row; turn **KNOB 1** while you hold it, one click one place,
  the other boxes sliding over to make room; let go and it lands. Every other key plays as usual, so you hear the
  effects while you route them.
- **Parallel or Chain.** **A#4** (a tap) or **KNOB 2** switches the mode; the small line at the top says which.
  - **Parallel** is ChoralRoot as it always was: three buses side by side, each fed by the parts' sends and added
    back into the mix. The effects' order is kept but not heard here. The **Filter** has two places: hold G#4 and turn
    KNOB 1 anticlockwise to put it **before** the buses (the effects hear the filtered signal, the dry sound is
    filtered too), clockwise to put it back **after** them, on the whole output, as before.
  - **Chain** is one path: the dry mix goes into the first block, each effect adds its sound to everything before it
    and passes it all on, and the filter filters everything that reached it. Chorus into Delay into Reverb makes the
    echoes shimmer and then washes them out; Reverb first gives a delay that repeats a whole hall. In Chain each
    effect's amount is the chord part's send into it (the Sends page); the bass part's own sends rest, the bass goes
    through the chain with the chords. The drive stays in each part's sound, before everything, in both modes.
- **Kept with the instrument.** Mode and order are set once, like the other effect settings, and kept by SAVE held.
- **The same sound unless you change it.** Parallel with the filter after the effects, where every FM-1 starts, is
  exactly 0.26.1: not one sample differs. On the FM-1 the reverb still runs on the second core in both modes.
- The manual's [Routing](../MANUAL.md#routing) section has the details. GitHub issue 21 asked for this; the design is
  [FX.md](../FX.md) "The routing".

## The device manager finds an FM-1 called "FM-1" (new in 0.27)

- On a Mac the web device manager could say "FM-1 not found" while the installer saw the FM-1 fine (GitHub issue 32).
  ChoralRoot names its USB port "ChoralRoot FM-1", but macOS keeps the name it first saw for a USB device, so an FM-1
  updated from the stock firmware stays "FM-1" in the computer's MIDI list. The manager now takes either name and
  checks, as it always did, that the port answers as ChoralRoot before it reads anything.

## Installing

- The web installer's default is 0.27: press Install. Options > Version reads `ChoralRoot 0.27`.
- **From 0.26.1 or 0.26** nothing needs doing: your sounds, loops, favorites and settings are all there, and the
  effects sound as they did until you open the Routing page.
- **From 0.25.1 or older**: install 0.27 as you would 0.26.1; [0.25.1.md](0.25.1.md) "Installing" says what the
  update from those changes (your FM-1 is moved to the FM-1 chord keys once).
- **A Single core package** for FM-1s that have trouble with Dual Core: the web installer's **Advanced > Hardware
  fallback**. Options > Version then reads `ChoralRoot 0.27 1-core`. The routing works the same on one core.
- **Signed builds**: as 0.26.1, GitHub built these packages from the tag's source and signed them;
  [0.26.1.md](0.26.1.md) "Checking a package" says how to check one (`choralroot-0.27.fwsc`).
- **The SMK-37**: a community test build of 0.27, `0.27-smk37`, with the routing, and K1's volume shown on the
  screen and remembered across power-off, GitHub issue 30 ([SMK37.md](../../SMK37.md)).

## Going back to 0.26.1

The installer offers 0.26.1 under Other versions.

- **Your settings, sounds, loops and favorites stay as they are**: 0.27 stores everything exactly as 0.26.1 does.
  The routing's mode and order are kept in the same record under two new tags that 0.26.1 skips, so it comes up in
  Parallel with the filter after the effects, its own sound.
- One exception: if you had moved the filter before the effects AND set every one of the filter's four cells off its
  default, 0.26.1 takes the effects' global settings (the reverb's, delay's and chorus's own parameters and the bass
  part's sends) back to their defaults once; your sounds, loops and the chord part's sends are untouched.
- The Routing page is not there; the FX layer has eight pages again.
