ChoralRoot 0.46 is for **ChoralRoot on the Ableton Move** (in RemoteFM1): **knobs you can control** in the takeover's
sound editor, FX and settings, and **the engine's name on the Move's screen while you pick one**. On the FM-1 and the
SMK-37 nothing changes: 0.46 plays exactly as 0.45, and your settings, sounds, loops, progressions and favorites come
through the update.

## On the Move (RemoteFM1)

- **Knobs that stay under control.** In the takeover, the knobs of the sound editor, the FX, the settings pages and
  the Options were far too quick: a slight nudge could take the Reverb's Size from 0 to nearly 127. They now move one
  step for every two clicks of a Move knob, and they speed up the way the FM-1's own knobs do: a slow turn makes the
  smallest change (a filter's cutoff 0.01 at a time), a fast spin sweeps the whole range in one turn. The lists (a
  sound, a mode, a scale) and Perf amount turn as before.
- **The engine's name while you pick it.** Holding OPT and turning PRESETS (or ALGORITHM for the bass) picks the
  sound engine. The Move's screen now names each engine as you turn past it (FM TONE, VA, ...), as the FM-1's screen
  does, not only the sound you land on when you let go of OPT.

## Going back

- **To 0.45**: the installer offers it under Other versions. Your settings, sounds, loops, progressions and favorites
  stay; the FM-1 plays the same. On the Move, RemoteFM1's 0.45 ChoralRoot has the quick knobs and names the engine only
  when you let go of OPT.
- **To another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader (MANUAL.md
  "Switching firmwares"); back up ChoralRoot's data in the installer first.
- **To the stock firmware completely**: M-VAVE's own updater, M-Updater.
