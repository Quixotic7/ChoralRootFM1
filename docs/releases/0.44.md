ChoralRoot 0.44 makes the screen **name the chord you play by hand** on the Full keyboard (GitHub issue 61), makes the
Keyboard view **light every note you hold**, and gives the device manager **Save loops as MIDI** (contributed by
Nikitos-U, GitHub pull request 50). On the SMK-37, 0.44-smk37 also brings **Bluetooth MIDI**. Your settings, sounds,
loops, progressions and favorites come through the update.

## The screen names your chords

- **The Full keyboard** (KEY page 2, Keyboard `Full`): play several notes together and the screen names the chord
  they make (C, Am7, F/A, ...), with the bass in orange, as a progression's chord is named. Before, it showed only the
  last note you pressed. The name changes when you press a key and stays while you let go; a single key is a single
  note again. Only the screen changes: nothing that sounds, is recorded or is sent over MIDI is different.
- **The Keyboard view** (and Geek Out) lights every note you are holding when you play single notes, on either
  keyboard (Key plays Notes, or the Full keyboard), not only the last one.
- The Chords keyboard, Key plays Chords and loops name their chords as before.

## Loops as MIDI (the device manager)

On the Loops tab, **Save loops as MIDI…** takes the selected slots (or library loops) and saves them as one Standard
MIDI File for a DAW: write the order (`1 2x2 3 4` plays loop 2 twice) and the tempo, and the line under them shows the
bars and the length. Chords go on channel 1 and the bass on channel 2, with a marker per loop and its time signature.
A loop's progression chords come from your FM-1's progressions, so keep it connected for those. What the FM-1 adds
live (the voicing, strum, arp and the other performance modes, the steps' ratchets, conditions and chance) is not in
the file: record the FM-1's MIDI out for that.

## On the SMK-37 (0.44-smk37)

- **Bluetooth MIDI**, as on the FM-1: Options > MIDI > Bluetooth `On`, then switch the keyboard off and on. It shows
  up as `ChoralRoot XXXX` on a computer, phone or tablet. The SMK-37 Pro and Elite have the radio. Thanks to everyone
  who tried the beta.
- The installer writes a small second resource region with the firmware, to make room for Bluetooth. Nothing to do:
  install as usual.
- The Full keyboard's chord names and the Keyboard view's held notes as on the FM-1.

## Going back

- **To 0.43**: the installer offers it under Other versions. Your settings, sounds, loops, progressions and favorites
  stay; the Full keyboard shows the last note again. On the SMK-37, 0.43-smk37 has no Bluetooth.
- **To another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader (MANUAL.md
  "Switching firmwares"); back up ChoralRoot's data in the installer first.
- **To the stock firmware completely**: M-VAVE's own updater, M-Updater.
