ChoralRoot 0.45 is a release of fixes: **knobs that keep up with a fast turn**, **steadier MIDI on the TRS input**,
**USB audio at 48 kHz without clicks on an iPhone**, **no automatic save while a computer records the FM-1**, a USB
Host that accepts the Elektron Digitone II, and smaller ones. All ten come from flariut (GitHub pull requests 67 to 76),
most of them ported from Felucca, the firmware ChoralRoot is built on. Thank you. Nothing about the sounds, chords, loops
or layout changes. Your settings, sounds, loops, progressions and favorites come through the update.

## The knobs

- **A fast turn no longer loses clicks.** A quick flick of a knob could skip a step, or not count at all. Every click
  now counts, however fast you turn.

## MIDI

- **The TRS MIDI input** no longer reads bytes that never came in, which could now and then give a stray or missing
  note.
- **An external MIDI clock on the TRS input** arrives evenly. Its pulses were sometimes delayed and bunched while the
  FM-1 was busy making sound, which made a loop that follows the clock waver.
- **USB Host**: a device that declares a large USB MIDI packet, such as an Elektron Digitone II, now works. Before, the
  FM-1 showed NO MIDI for it.

## USB audio and USB

- **48 kHz without clicks.** At 48 kHz (Options > USB Rate `44.1 + 48 kHz`), the FM-1 now follows the computer's or the
  phone's clock exactly. An iPhone filming video with the FM-1 as its microphone heard a click every few seconds; that
  is gone. 44.1 kHz is unchanged.
- **No automatic save while the FM-1 is being recorded.** While an app records the FM-1's audio over USB, a change
  you make (an Option, a favorite) waits and is stored a moment after the recording app lets go of the FM-1, as it
  already waited while a loop plays. A save would have left a click in the recording. Holding SAVE still saves at
  once.
- **The web editor and the device manager** no longer miss a request now and then, so a backup or a sound send does
  not stall.
- **USB requests** that arrive back to back are answered at once, so a computer's setup of the FM-1 does not wait
  for a timeout.

## On the screen and the panel

- **The popups with a stripe per value** (the octave, the split point, the progression keys) light one more stripe
  per step. The octave used to skip a stripe on its way down from 0.
- **The panel no longer blinks** when the FM-1 saves: a column of keys lit up for an instant at each automatic save.

## On the SMK-37 (0.45-smk37)

The MIDI, USB and screen fixes above, as on the FM-1. The knob fix and the panel blink were the FM-1's own:
the SMK-37's controls and lights are read and lit another way.

## Going back

- **To 0.44**: the installer offers it under Other versions. Your settings, sounds, loops, progressions and favorites
  stay; the fixes above go with it.
- **To another firmware**: [M-Flasher](https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher) with the M-Bootloader (MANUAL.md
  "Switching firmwares"); back up ChoralRoot's data in the installer first.
- **To the stock firmware completely**: M-VAVE's own updater, M-Updater.
