**USB audio recording from Melodee** (draft): the computer can now record the FM-1 over USB, class compliant, no driver needed. The device **ChoralRoot In** gives six channels at 44.1 kHz: the master (without the metronome click), the CHORD part and the BASS part as stereo pairs (the parts dry, before the FX, at −6 dB). Options > **USB Record** (On by default) switches it off (the FM-1 reconnects itself for a second so the computer sees the change); Options > **USB Level** = Fixed records at the full level while MASTER sets only the speaker and headphones (Felucca 1.0.5's USB LEVEL FIXED). With USB Record Off, and in SAFE MODE, the FM-1 presents its serial console instead. Melodee's playback device (the computer's audio through the FM-1) is not included. The MIDI port is now called "ChoralRoot FM-1". See docs/USB-AUDIO.md (what the computer sees, the channels, the limits). Felucca's earlier master-only USB input is replaced by ChoralRoot In's first two channels.

**BASS+ fix** (Felucca 1.0.5, #42): the speaker's BASS+ mode (a Felucca setting a migrated Felucca record can carry) no longer cancels up to 6 dB of the mix's 300–600 Hz: its low-pass has four poles. Nothing else sounds different.

**CZ-1 from Melodee** (by Kerem Kilic): engine 14, native Casio CZ-1 tones with their eight-step pitch, timbre and volume envelopes, Casio's 64 preset tones, eight 16-tone banks, Casio tone SysEx in and out over USB MIDI (the handshake's partial frames are now sent as continuing SysEx), the tone kept with the user slot and in backups; editor pages with an envelope band, five chord rows and a bass in the bank (docs/CZ1.md).

**SAFE MODE**: two crashes in a row within 30 s of starting (power cycles do not count) start ChoralRoot in SAFE MODE: factory sounds, the stored data ignored and untouched, installing and backing up still work, Options > Flash Data erases the data (OCT+ twice). Only two more crashes there fall back to ROM boot. The console's `boot` command and the GEEK OUT view show where a crashed boot stopped.

**The loop's corner dial**: while a loop plays, records or waits for REC, the screen shows a small dial at the right end of the top line (red progress from 12 o'clock, a pulse on the downbeat) instead of Orchid's ring round every screen, menu and popup. REC armed puts a red dot in the dial's middle, lit like the REC LED; while the first loop records the dial's track turns red and the dot blinks with REC; an overdub blinks the dot over the playing loop. The ring stays only where the loop is the whole screen (the count-in, undo), and nothing is drawn in the sound editor or on the Options pages (the REC and LOOP LEDs say it there).

**.syx export and import**: an FM6 slot's voice travels as a DX7 single-voice .syx (Dexed and DX7 editors read it; banks import too, pick the voice) and a CZ-1 slot's tone as a Casio tone dump, from the installer's Sounds section (Export .syx, Import) and `fm1_install.py --export-syx / --import-syx [--voice]`; an imported voice gets a sound record of the engine's defaults under the voice's name.

**FM TONE** (engine 15, `eng_quad.c`, code name QUAD): a new four-operator FM engine in the style of Elektron's Digitone (our own code), between FM6 and VA in the engine list: eight algorithms over operators C, A, B1, B2, ratios as the Digitone steps them (Ratio B one knob over the pair), harmonics, detune, feedback, a mix of two outputs, two operator envelopes, a multimode filter with its envelope and a base-width filter, its own amp envelope and three LFOs; 16 starting presets (EP, BELL, BASS, ... NOISE-ISH). The editor shows its pages as the approved screens: the algorithm diagram over SYN 1, the two operator envelopes side by side, the base-width window on the filter curve, one screen per LFO with the wave drawn from its start phase. Aligned with the Digitone manual (Appendix A): its eight algorithms, drawn as the manual draws them (a carrier at full level on a dotted line, one under its operator envelope on a solid line), the carrier levels and the B LEV law, HARM −26..+26, Ratio B's two hands as the Digitone's (a detent steps B2, the top of the fraction; OPT / Shift steps B1, "fine · B1"), DTUNE and FDBK, key scaling for A, B1 and B2, LFO multipliers synced to the tempo or at a fixed 120 BPM, a bipolar fade (in or out), phase reset per operator group (Off, All, C, A+B, A+B2), and the filter chain (the base-width filter first, then Off, LP12, HP12 or LP24). Patches are stored as version 3; ones saved by earlier 0.14 builds still load. An edited FM TONE sound keeps its patch in its user slot, in backups (a new object, 22, "FM TONE patches") and as a sound file (patch kind `quad`; there is no .syx for FM TONE).

**ANALOG retired; the engine order**: the VA does what ANALOG did, better, so ANALOG leaves ChoralRoot (its code is out of the image). A sound you saved on ANALOG loads on the VA with its filter, drive, mix, detune and envelope settings carried over (a message says so), at the end of the VA's list; save it again to keep it as a VA sound. The engines are now, on the picker's roots D4..G5: FM6, FM TONE, VA, CZ-1, PHASE, LOFI, VOICE, TRIO, WHEEL, PHYS, NOISE: eleven engines, a root each again. The bass's default sound is the VA's DEEP SUB.

**Sounds as files**: the installer page has a Sounds section and `tools/fm1_install.py` gained `--sounds`, `--export-sound`, `--import-sound`, `--rename-sound` and `--delete-sound`: a single user sound (its record and, for VA / FM6 / CZ-1, its patch) travels as a small `choralroot-sound` JSON file, into any of the 32 slots, with no restart of the FM-1 (docs/SOUNDS.md). The backup protocol was run on hardware for the first time; the installer's list of backup objects now includes the FM6 patch stores and the CZ-1 stores and banks on the command line too.

**Presets per engine** (docs/PRESETS.md): PRESETS now turns through the presets of the engine the chord part plays, and nothing else: 00 INIT, the engine's factory presets, then the ones you saved into it; the popup says where you are ("FM6 · 05/26"). Hold OPT and turn PRESETS to change the engine; each engine remembers the preset you were on. ALGORITHM does the same for the bass part's engine (00 is still OFF). SAVE asks **Overwrite** or **Save as new**: overwrite any preset, a factory one included (it gets a small square after its name and can be reset to factory by holding SAVE for a second in the dialog), or save a new one at the end of the engine's list and name it; a user preset is deleted the same way. Your 32 user slots are the storage: sounds saved by 0.13 appear at the end of their engine's list. ChoralRoot's curated 48-sound list is gone (its sounds are all still there, in their engines; FM6 gains PIANO as preset 25). In the PERF and FX layers the root keys now play the chord (SELECT picks the mode or the effect), so a change is heard at once; the LOOP layer's Quantize "none" no longer draws a grid. Settings record version 6: the sound you play and the place per engine are stored; an older record starts on TINE EP and DEEP SUB (version 7: a record whose bass was ANALOG's SUB BASS too).

Settings record version 5 (USB Record, USB Level); older records keep everything and take the defaults (USB Record on, Master); a 0.14 dev build's USB Audio Out byte is cleared.
