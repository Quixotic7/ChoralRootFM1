**USB audio from Melodee** (draft): the FM-1 is now also a class-compliant USB audio interface, no driver needed. **ChoralRoot Out** plays the computer through the FM-1 (added after ChoralRoot's master limiter, set by the MASTER knob); **ChoralRoot In** records six channels at 44.1 kHz: the master (without the metronome click and without the computer's own playback), the CHORD part and the BASS part as stereo pairs (the parts dry, before the FX, at −6 dB). Options > **USB Audio Out** and **USB Audio In** switch each device off (the FM-1 reconnects itself for a second so the computer sees the change); Options > **USB Level** = Fixed records at the full level while MASTER sets only the speaker and headphones (Felucca 1.0.5's USB LEVEL FIXED). With both devices off, and in SAFE MODE, the FM-1 presents its serial console instead. The MIDI port is now called "ChoralRoot FM-1". See docs/USB-AUDIO.md (what the computer sees, the channels, the limits). Felucca's earlier master-only USB input is replaced by ChoralRoot In's first two channels.

**BASS+ fix** (Felucca 1.0.5, #42): the speaker's BASS+ mode (a Felucca setting a migrated Felucca record can carry) no longer cancels up to 6 dB of the mix's 300–600 Hz: its low-pass has four poles. Nothing else sounds different.

**CZ-1 from Melodee** (by Kerem Kilic): engine 14, native Casio CZ-1 tones with their eight-step pitch, timbre and volume envelopes, Casio's 64 preset tones, eight 16-tone banks, Casio tone SysEx in and out over USB MIDI (the handshake's partial frames are now sent as continuing SysEx), the tone kept with the user slot and in backups; editor pages with an envelope band, five chord rows and a bass in the bank (docs/CZ1.md).

**SAFE MODE**: two crashes in a row within 30 s of starting (power cycles do not count) start ChoralRoot in SAFE MODE: factory sounds, the stored data ignored and untouched, installing and backing up still work, Options > Flash Data erases the data (OCT+ twice). Only two more crashes there fall back to ROM boot. The console's `boot` command and the GEEK OUT view show where a crashed boot stopped.

Settings record version 4 (USB Audio Out / In, USB Level); older records keep everything and take the defaults (both devices on, Master).
